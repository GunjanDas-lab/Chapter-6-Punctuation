,

1. Comma ( , )

  • To separate items in a list:
    Example: I bought apples, oranges, bananas, and grapes.
  • Before conjunctions in compound sentences:
    Example: I wanted to go for a walk, but it started to rain.
  • After introductory phrases or clauses:
    Example: After dinner, we went for a stroll.
  • To set off non-essential information:
    Example: My brother, who lives in London, is visiting us.
;

2. Semicolon ( ; )

  • To connect closely related independent clauses:
    Example: I have a big test tomorrow; I can’t go out tonight.
  • Before conjunctive adverbs (however, therefore, moreover):
    Example: She didn’t see the step; therefore, she tripped.
:

3. Colon ( : )

  • To introduce a list or a quote:
    Example: You need to buy the following: bread, milk, and eggs.
  • Before an explanation or elaboration:
    Example: There was one thing I couldn’t understand: why he left so suddenly.
’

4. Apostrophe ( ’ )

  • To indicate possession:
    Example: The dog’s leash is red.
  • In contractions:
    Example: It’s a sunny day.
!

5. Exclamation Mark ( ! )

  • To express strong emotion or emphasis:
    Example: Watch out!
?

6. Question Mark ( ? )

  • At the end of a direct question:
    Example: What time is the meeting?
“ ”

7. Quotation Marks ( “ ” )

  • To enclose direct speech or quotations:
    Example: She said, “I will be late.”
